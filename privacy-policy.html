1. Who we are

S.L.A Fruit Shop is a family-run fruit shop operated by Shahjahan, established in 2009, at ECR Road, Kattumavadi, Tamil Nadu 614630, India. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us using the details in section 13.

2. What we collect

We only collect what the website or app needs to work. In detail:

Account details

If you create an account, we store your name, email address, an optional phone number and your password. Passwords are stored only as a bcrypt hash; we never store or see your plain password. If you sign in with Facebook, Instagram or X (Twitter), we receive your name, email address, the provider's account ID and, where the provider supplies it, a profile picture URL. We do not receive your social media password.

Order details

When you place an order we store your name, email address (when supplied), phone number, delivery address, the items and quantities you ordered, the total amount, your chosen payment method and your order and payment status. Orders are linked to your account by an internal account ID.

Contact form details

If you use the contact form we store your name, phone number, the service you selected, an optional address and your message. Contact form entries are not linked to a customer account.

Information kept on your device

The website uses your browser's local storage. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us except where noted:

Cookies

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. When you sign in with a social provider, a short-lived session cookie (named connect.sid, HTTP-only, expiring after about 10 minutes) is used only to complete the sign-in handshake. Embedded services described in section 5 and section 11 may set their own cookies according to their policies.

Server logs and notifications

Our server writes operational error messages to its console. If the optional WhatsApp, Telegram or email notification services are not configured, the notification content (which can include a customer's name, phone number, email address and order details) may be written to the server console instead. We do not run third-party analytics, tracking pixels or advertising SDKs.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, calendar data or advertising identifiers.

3. Payments

We do not use a third-party payment gateway, and we do not collect or store card numbers, UPI PINs, CVV codes or bank passwords. UPI payments are made directly from your UPI app to the shop's UPI ID shown at checkout. Net Banking is simulated in the app for demonstration and Cash on Delivery is available. What we record is the payment method, the shop's UPI ID or the bank name you typed, and the payment status. Please check the amount and the payee in your own UPI or banking app before approving any payment.

4. Why we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, sign you in and let you reset your password.
  • To take, prepare, deliver and keep a record of your orders.
  • To contact you about an order or a message you sent us.
  • To operate the site safely, diagnose errors and prevent abuse.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or profiling.

6. How long we keep it

The application does not currently run an automated retention schedule. In practice:

  • Account: kept until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.
  • Orders: kept as a business and accounting record. If you delete your account, the order's name, phone, email, address and payment identifiers are removed or replaced, while the item list, amounts and dates remain in anonymised form.
  • Contact messages: kept until the enquiry has been handled and is no longer needed, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Password-reset links: expire after 1 hour.
  • Sign-in token: expires after 7 days (or sooner if you sign out or delete your account).
  • Social sign-in session cookie: expires after about 10 minutes.
  • Cart, language and install-prompt data on your device: kept until you clear your browser storage.

7. How we protect it

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, password-reset tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes, sign-in tokens are signed and time-limited, and secrets are kept in environment variables rather than in the source code. The live site is served over HTTPS by our host. Account deletion requests are protected by re-authentication, a short-lived anti-forgery token and attempt limits.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately using section 13.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your personal information, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to certain uses. You can:

  • Delete your account yourself from the delete account page.
  • Ask us to delete a contact-form message or an order record, or to correct your details, using the contact details below.
  • Clear the cart, saved language and sign-in data at any time by clearing your browser storage or signing out.

Because contact-form messages are not linked to accounts, please include the name, phone number and approximate date you used so we can find the right record. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Deleting your account

Signed-in customers can delete their account at any time from the delete account page (also linked from the account menu on the home page). For local accounts you confirm with your current password; for social sign-in accounts you confirm with the account's email address. In both cases you must also type DELETE.

Deleting your account removes your account record and your sign-in token stops working. Your past orders are anonymised rather than erased so that sales and accounting records stay accurate. If you have never created an account but sent us a contact message, use the contact details below to ask for its deletion.

10. Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Third-party links and embeds

The site embeds a Google Map and loads fonts and code libraries from third-party servers. It also links to external sites such as Google Maps and Wikimedia Commons for photo credits. Those services receive your IP address and browser details when your browser loads them, and they follow their own privacy policies. We do not control their practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or the law changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will post a clear notice on this site.
